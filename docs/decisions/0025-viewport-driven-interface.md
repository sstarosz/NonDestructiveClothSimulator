# 0025. The work happens in the views, not in forms

**Date:** 14 November 2026 (after drawing the sleeve by hand in the first window)

## Context

Making a pattern through a list of panels and fields "felt like Excel" and was
too complex.

## Decision

Work happens on the table and in the 3D view, with a small shelf of tools. The
docks (Attribute Editor, Parameters) only show and fine-tune. Little text on
screen: no explanations, units on every number, hints in the help line. What a
formula drives is locked against dragging, for now.

## Why

Pattern making is visual; a tool should feel like a tool.

## What we gave up

Doing everything from one form, which is quicker to build.
