# 0016. Rejected: ordering the cloth's edges by graph colouring

**Date:** 26 November 2026
**Issues/PRs:** #104, PR #105

## Context

Ordering the edges so that none waits for the last made the cloth step about
30 % faster.

## Decision

Not done.

## Why

It stretched the cloth by 12 to 44 %.

## What we gave up

That 30 %. Try again only with a way to keep the stretch, measured on the
trousers.
